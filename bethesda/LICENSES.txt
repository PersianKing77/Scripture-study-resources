OTHER MATERIAL IN THIS PRODUCT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Scripture text — this product quotes only the American Standard Version
(1901). The 1901 American Standard Version is in the public domain in the
United States and is used throughout this edition. The King James Version is
discussed by name in the notes on the text (to explain a manuscript
difference) but is never quoted at length.

Ancient sources (Josephus, Eusebius, Ben Sira, the Copper Scroll, the
pilgrim itineraries) — the originals are public domain. No modern
copyrighted translation is quoted; every reference is a description in
this project's own words.

Excavation plans — Mauss (1888) and the Palestine Exploration Fund
surveys are public domain. The Ecole Biblique reports of 1957-62 and
later re-examinations remain in copyright; the footprints here are
schematic reconstructions drawn in this project's own coordinate frame,
not scans or traces.

Aerial imagery — NOT INCLUDED. This build ships with no default imagery
endpoint. The optional overlay is inert until the user supplies the URL
of a tile service they hold a commercial licence to use; no imagery
provider is bundled, fetched by default, or redistributed in these files.

Photographs — none included. The two frames ship empty.

Essays, reconstructions, handout, discussion questions and self-check —
created for this project.

Runtime page components supplied by the platform that built this project —
confirm their distribution terms with that platform. See
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt for the full runtime dependency inventory
(React, ReactDOM, Babel) and their licences.
