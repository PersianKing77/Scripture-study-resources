Privacy

A plain account of what this tool stores and what it sends over the network.

Notes, bookmarks, and settings are stored only in your browser (localStorage). All Gospel scripture text (World English Bible) is bundled with the app and requires no network request.
Requests to Google Fonts and unpkg (React, ReactDOM, Babel), needed to load the app itself, transmit ordinary web-request data — your IP address, user agent, and timestamp — to those services.
This build does not create accounts and does not include analytics.
Clearing your browser's site data for this tool deletes your local notes, bookmarks, settings, and any cached scripture text.

Author · Teacher · Tour leader · Scripture scholar

Taylor Halverson is a scripture scholar, educator, author, and creator of learning tools. He formerly taught entrepreneurship and innovation at Brigham Young University.

He is the author and editor of more than fifty books and nearly a thousand articles and blog posts, and a builder of scripture study resources: the ScripturePlus app with Scripture Central, whose scripture YouTube channel he co-hosted to more than fifty million views, and BYU's Virtual Scripture Group, whose team spent more than five years reconstructing New Testament Jerusalem in three dimensions.
