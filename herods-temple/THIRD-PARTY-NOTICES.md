## Reconstruction and text

The reconstruction, the geometry, the forty-three location commentaries, the guided tours,
the sight lines, the certainty and phase overlays, the printed study guide and the reading
index are the work of Taylor Halverson.

The geometry, textures and ambient sound are generated in code at run time.

## Ancient sources

- Mishnah, tractate *Middot*, for the plan and the dimensions of the courts, with *Tamid*,
  *Yoma*, *Shekalim*, *Sukkah* and *Parah* for the daily and festival service.
- Flavius Josephus, *The Jewish War* and *Jewish Antiquities*, for elevations, the
  porticoes and the fabric of the building.
- The Babylonian Talmud, for the service and for the memory of the second temple.
- The New Testament, for the events set in these courts.

These works are in the public domain. The descriptions in this product are the author's
own, and cite each source by book, chapter and line.

## Archaeology and scholarship

Excavated dimensions of the Royal Stoa, the southern stairway, the walls and the gates,
and the building phases of the platform, follow Leen Ritmeyer, *The Quest: Revealing the
Temple Mount in Jerusalem* (Jerusalem: Carta, 2006) and his published articles.

Those excavations are the work of Benjamin Mazar and the Hebrew University expedition to
the area south and west of the Temple Mount, 1968–1978, and of Ronny Reich and Eli Shukron
for the Herodian street and the stepped ascent.

The geometry in this model was drawn from those published measurements.

## Remote services

This application loads three.js modules from unpkg.com and typefaces from Google Fonts. Those
providers receive standard web-request information, such as IP address, browser information,
requested resource, and time of request. This product itself does not use analytics, accounts,
advertising trackers, or form submissions.

## Fonts and network requests

EB Garamond and Cormorant Garamond are currently loaded from Google Fonts
(fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) rather than bundled locally; three.js and its
add-ons are loaded from unpkg.com on every launch. See "Remote services" above for what
that discloses.

## Curriculum index

The reading index maps scripture reading blocks to locations in the model, and is keyed to
the reading block rather than to a published week number, since those are renumbered each
year. It covers the Old Testament and the New Testament, the two volumes whose reading
meets this building.

The Weekly Reading index is a generic reading-block guide and does not name any single
publisher's curriculum. This product is not affiliated with, authorised by, endorsed by or
sponsored by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints or Intellectual Reserve, Inc.
