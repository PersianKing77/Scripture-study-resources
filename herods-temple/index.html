    Herod's Temple Beta

    Prepared by Taylor Halverson, Ph.D.

    An explorable reconstruction of the Temple Mount as it stood in the time of Christ, c. 30 CE — the platform and its walls, the outer court and porticoes, the Court of the Women, the Court of Israel, the altar and laver, the sanctuary, the veil, and the Holy of Holies with the Foundation Stone within it.

    Every location is annotated from the King James New Testament, Josephus, the Mishnah and the Talmud, with notes where the sources disagree. Forty-three locations, one hundred sixty-two passages.

    Beta

    A work in progress. The reconstruction follows the majority reading of the sources; dimensions, decoration and the lines of the city walls remain debated. Where the sources conflict, the model shows the majority reconstruction and the location's note records the dissent rather than quietly settling it.

    On the measurements

    Dimensions come from the Mishnah's tractate Middot, with the cubit taken at 0.5 m — a round figure within the range scholars propose — and cross-checked against Josephus. Small differences of a cubit or two are inside the margin of the sources themselves.

    Moving around

    • Orbit surveys the whole site from above: drag to turn, scroll to zoom. The compass keeps you oriented — the facade genuinely faces the rising sun.
    • Walk in puts you on the ground: WASD to move, drag or ← → to look, Shift to run. On a phone a drive pad appears at the corner.
    • All 43 places lists every annotated location; ‹ Prev and Next › step through them in order, and Esc closes whatever is open.

    Seeing inside

    • Roofs and Cutaway lift the porticoes and open the sanctuary so the Holy Place and the Holy of Holies can be seen from outside.
    • Labels pins the names to the model; City adds Jerusalem around the mount — Olivet, Siloam, Golgotha, Hinnom — and can be turned off on a slower device.
    • Plan shows a small schematic of the precinct, north up, with a wedge marking where you stand and which way you face — useful when the courts start to look alike.
    • Morning cycles the hour: dawn, morning, noon, dusk. The light is the same light the pilgrims climbed in.

    Reading and teaching

    • Each panel carries the KJV passages for that place, a scholarly note, its sources, and “where the sources differ.”
    • Begin runs a guided sequence — the full tour, the Day of Atonement, a Passover pilgrimage, or Into greater light, which climbs the gradient of access from the open outer court to the Holy of Holies — visiting the places in the order the day required.
    • Handout opens the whole study guide, all 43 locations and 162 passages, ready to print or save as PDF.
    • Copy link gives a link straight to one place, in orbit or on foot, to paste into a lesson.

    Sources

    • Dimensions and courts: Mishnah Middot, with Tamid, Yoma and Talmudic material for the service.
    • Elevations, porticoes and the fabric of the building: Josephus, Jewish War and Antiquities.
    • Scripture quoted from the King James Version.
